For members

Book a court in seconds, from anywhere

No phone calls and no paper list at the clubhouse. Members see what is free and book it themselves.

  • 🎾

    Court booking

    Pick a court and a time and add your partners. The club's rules for opening hours, prime time and quotas are applied automatically.

  • 🔁

    Recurring bookings

    Reserve the same slot every week or every few weeks. Change or cancel one date, or all following ones.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧

    Families

    Parents book and manage on behalf of their children.

  • 📅

    Club calendar

    Work efforts, parties, tournaments and meetings, with registration and head counts. Share an event by link or WhatsApp.

  • 🔔

    Notifications

    Booking added or cancelled, a reminder before the match, new events and news. By push, by email, or not at all.

  • 🏆

    Statistics and badges

    Who plays the most and with whom, leaderboards, and badges earned over the season.

For the board

The whole club in one place

Less paperwork for the people who run the club in their spare time.

  • 👥

    Members and seasons

    Member list, roles such as sports director, treasurer or trainer, and enrollment per season.

  • 🏟️

    Courts and rules

    Courts, opening hours, prime time, play modes and who may book what.

  • 🚧

    Court blockings

    Block courts for tournaments, maintenance, weather or weekly training. Affected players are informed.

  • 🎟️

    Guest cards

    Let guests book with a link or a code and a fixed number of bookings.

  • 📣

    News and emails

    Publish club news with attachments and send it by email to all members, the youth or selected roles. Every email text can be adapted.

  • 📈

    Club statistics

    Court utilization and activity over the season.

Open source

Your club's data is not locked in

The complete source code of Clubnetz is public under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can read it, improve it and run it on your own server.

One Docker image runs the whole app. It needs a PostgreSQL database and an SMTP server for emails.

  • ASP.NET Core
  • Blazor WebAssembly
  • PostgreSQL
  • Docker

Hosting guide on GitHub

git clone https://github.com/sleepwalkerffs/Clubnetz.git
cd Clubnetz
docker build -t clubnetz .

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Clubnetz really free?

Yes. Clubnetz is free for clubs and for members, and it contains no advertising. The source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Is Clubnetz only for tennis clubs?

It was built for a tennis club and tennis is what it is tested with every day. It works for any club that has courts to book and members to manage.

Do members have to install an app?

No. Clubnetz runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Members who want to can add it to their home screen, where it behaves like an app and can send push notifications.

Can we host Clubnetz ourselves?

Yes. The repository contains a Dockerfile that builds one image for the whole app. You need a PostgreSQL database and an SMTP server for emails.

What about data protection?

The hosted Clubnetz runs on servers in the EU. Members can export their personal data, leave a club and delete their account themselves.

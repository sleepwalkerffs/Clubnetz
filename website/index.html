Club management and court booking for tennis clubs

Clubnetz is a free, open-source web app. Members book a court in a few taps on their phone and pay their membership fee online. The board manages members, seasons, fees, guests, events and news in one place.

A look inside

This is what your club gets

Real screens of a club with more than 600 members. Only the names are invented.

Every court and every booking at a glance

The booking grid shows all courts of a day with play modes, prime time, blocked courts and your own matches. Tap a free slot to book it, partners included.

  • Opening hours, prime time and quotas are checked automatically
  • Weekly series, court blockings and tournaments in the same grid
  • All upcoming club events in the calendar

New

Membership fees without the spreadsheet

The club enters its price list once per season. Members choose their plan, for themselves or the whole family, and pay online. The treasurer sees at a glance who has paid and who has not.

  • Card and SEPA direct debit through the club's own Stripe account
  • A paid fee enrolls the members in the season
  • Bank transfers and cash recorded by hand, export to Excel

Events people actually sign up for

Work efforts, parties, tournaments and meetings with registration, head count and the organizer's questions. News reach members by push notification or email.

  • "Sausage or steak?" answered right in the registration
  • Share an event by link or WhatsApp
  • Pinned news, attachments and expiry dates

A season to look back on

The board sees how the courts are used, hour by hour and court by court. Members collect badges by playing and keep them in their trophy case.

  • Occupancy, prime time share and the busiest days
  • Comparison with last season at the same point
  • Leaderboards, tier badges and special awards

A member list that stays up to date

Members keep their own details current. The board filters by season, age group and role, and copies email addresses for its mailings.

  • Who is new this season and who has not come back yet
  • Families, roles and season enrollment in one place
  • Other members only see name and profile picture

Open source

Your club's data is not locked in

The complete source code of Clubnetz is public under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can read it, improve it and run it on your own server.

One Docker image runs the whole app. It needs a PostgreSQL database and an SMTP server for emails.

  • ASP.NET Core
  • Blazor WebAssembly
  • PostgreSQL
  • Docker

Hosting guide on GitHub

git clone https://github.com/sleepwalkerffs/Clubnetz.git
cd Clubnetz
docker build -t clubnetz .

For tinkerers

An API for your own ideas

Somebody in your club knows a little programming? With an API key, your own scripts and tools can read and change what the app shows: bookings, members, events, news and court blockings.

  • Show free courts or the next events on your club website
  • Export members to a spreadsheet or your accounting tool
  • Read-only keys for displays and exports
  • Each key belongs to one club and can be revoked at any time

Read the API documentation

# How many members do we have?
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer cnz_..." \
  https://my.clubnetz.app/api/Clubs/12/Members/summary

# Which courts can be booked?
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer cnz_..." \
  https://my.clubnetz.app/api/Clubs/12/Courts

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Clubnetz really free?

Yes. Clubnetz is free for clubs and for members, and it contains no advertising. The source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Is Clubnetz only for tennis clubs?

It was built for a tennis club and tennis is what it is tested with every day. It works for any club that has courts to book and members to manage.

Do members have to install an app?

No. Clubnetz runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Members who want to can add it to their home screen, where it behaves like an app and can send push notifications.

Can we host Clubnetz ourselves?

Yes. The repository contains a Dockerfile that builds one image for the whole app. You need a PostgreSQL database and an SMTP server for emails.

What about data protection?

The hosted Clubnetz runs on servers in the EU. Other members only see your name and profile picture. Members can export their personal data, leave a club and delete their account themselves.

Can members pay their membership fee online?

Yes, if the club wants to. The club enters its price list as membership plans, members choose a plan for themselves or their family and pay by card or SEPA direct debit. The money goes straight to the club's own Stripe account, and a paid fee enrolls the members in the season. Bank transfers and cash are recorded by the treasurer.

Is there an API?

Yes. Club admins create API keys in the app and use them to read and change the data of their club from their own scripts and tools, for example to show free courts on the club website or to export the member list. API documentation
